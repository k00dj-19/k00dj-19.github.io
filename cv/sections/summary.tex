\cvsection{Summary}

\begin{cvparagraph}
Integrated M.S.--Ph.D. student at \textbf{KAIST AI} (DAVIAN Lab), advised by Prof.\ \href{https://sites.google.com/site/jaegulchoo/}{Jaegul Choo}.
I build \textbf{Vision-Language-Action (VLA)} models for robot manipulation, working on how to ground policies in the \textbf{spatial structure} of a scene --- 3D trajectories, robot-centric pointmaps --- building on earlier work localizing sound sources in visual scenes.
Alongside this, I work on \textbf{LLM safety}: content moderation for specialized domains and defense for LLM agents.
\end{cvparagraph}
