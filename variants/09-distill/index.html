About

I am an integrated M.S.–Ph.D. student in the DAVIAN Lab at KAIST AI, advised by Professor Jaegul Choo. My research interests include Vision-Language-Action (VLA), LLM Safety, and various multi-modal learning tasks.

Previously, I completed my B.S. in Computer Science & Engineering at Kyung Hee University, where I was an undergraduate researcher in the Visual AI Lab under Prof. Jung Uk Kim. During that time I published four papers at top-tier venues (CVPR × 2, AAAI, ACM MM).
